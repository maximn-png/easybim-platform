GSCreatedGal Shem Tov · 21 Jan 2024MNLast updatedMaxim Nikolsky · 17 May 2024
A Common Data Environment (CDE) is a shared digital space where all project information is stored and managed throughout the lifecycle.
The CDE lets project teams collaborate with a single source of truth for all documentation, models and data.
ISO 19650 defines four states: Work in Progress, Shared, Published, and Archived.
